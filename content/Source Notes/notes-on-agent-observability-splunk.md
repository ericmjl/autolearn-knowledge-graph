## What it says

Splunk argues that agents need more than one check: evaluation, observability, tokenomics, and guardrails on a single platform, run across 100% of production traffic rather than a sampled slice. Eval-G1, a purpose-built small language model, scores and protects traffic at a fraction of the cost of a frontier judge, which makes always-on evaluation economically viable. Failures are traced back to cause, and evaluations are converted into runtime guardrails that block or steer risky actions instead of flagging problems after the fact.

## Claims drawn from this source

- **Evaluation of every agent interaction is now economically viable.** Purpose-built Eval-G1 evaluation models score and protect 100% of production traffic at a fraction of the cost of a frontier judge; evaluation and guardrails run on every interaction, not a sampled slice.
- **Agents need evaluation, observability, tokenomics, and guardrails together.** Splunk Agent Observability brings together evaluation, observability, tokenomics, and guardrails so teams can prove agents are right, know what they cost, and block the actions they shouldn't take.
- **Full-fidelity tracing pinpoints cause, not symptom.** It traces every step of a workflow from request to response, reads latency and errors next to quality and security signals like hallucinations, bias, prompt injection, and PII leakage, and shortens root-cause analysis from days to minutes.
- **General-purpose monitoring cannot see agent behavior.** General-purpose monitoring does not see an agent's reasoning, tool calls, output quality, and more.
- **Evaluations become runtime guardrails.** The platform turns the evaluations you trust into runtime guardrails that block or steer risky actions in real time rather than flagging problems after the fact.
- **Token cost is tracked per agent and workflow.** Splunk tracks token cost by agent and workflow and ties AI cost to the value it delivers.
