## What it says

The Wikipedia disambiguation page for 'Test' catalogs what the term covers: an educational assessment intended to measure a respondent's knowledge or abilities, software testing (computing tests, the Unix test command, the TEST x86 instruction), and engineering tests (metal, mechanical, product, stress, system, proof, and equipment tests). It also lists experimental and statistical uses (Experiment, a procedure carried out in order to test a hypothesis; Statistical hypothesis test) and the sporting, law, and entertainment senses. The page thus frames testing as a family of scoped judgments rather than a single activity.

## Claims drawn from this source

- **Test as an assessment measures knowledge or abilities.** Test (assessment) is 'an educational assessment intended to measure the respondents' knowledge or other abilities'.
- **Computing tests are part of the same family.** The page lists '.test, a reserved top-level domain', 'Software testing', 'test (Unix), a Unix command for evaluating conditional expressions', and 'TEST (x86 instruction), an x86 assembly language instruction'.
- **Engineering testing spans distinct scopes.** The page names Metal testing, Mechanical testing (Proof test, a stress test to demonstrate the fitness of a load-bearing structure), Product testing, Stress testing, System testing, and Test equipment as separate entries.
- **Statistics treats inference as a test.** The page lists 'Experiment, a procedure carried out in order to test a hypothesis' and 'Statistical hypothesis test, techniques to reach conclusions about probabilistic behavior'.
