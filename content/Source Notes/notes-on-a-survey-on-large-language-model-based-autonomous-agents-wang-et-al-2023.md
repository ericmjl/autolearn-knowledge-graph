## What it says

This source is a survey of LLM-based autonomous agents that argues these agents are a promising direction because LLMs acquire vast web knowledge and can approach human-level intelligence, unlike earlier agents trained with limited knowledge in isolated environments. It presents a unified framework for constructing such agents, reviews applications across social science, natural science, and engineering, and covers evaluation strategies, challenges, and future directions. The authors also maintain a repository of relevant references to keep the survey current.

## Claims drawn from this source

- **LLM agents differ from earlier isolated-environment agents.** The survey contrasts LLM-based agents with prior work that trained agents with limited knowledge in isolated environments, which diverges from human learning and makes human-like decisions hard.
- **LLMs provide broad web knowledge.** The source says LLMs have demonstrated remarkable potential in achieving human-level intelligence through acquisition of vast amounts of web knowledge.
- **A unified construction framework is proposed.** The paper proposes a unified framework for constructing LLM-based autonomous agents that encompasses a majority of previous work.
- **Applications span multiple fields.** The survey gives a comprehensive overview of diverse applications in social science, natural science, and engineering.
- **Evaluation strategies are reviewed.** The source delves into evaluation strategies commonly used for LLM-based autonomous agents.
- **Challenges and future directions are identified.** Based on prior studies, the survey presents several challenges and future directions in the field.
- **The survey is maintained via a reference repository.** The authors maintain a repository of relevant references to track the field and continuously update the survey.
